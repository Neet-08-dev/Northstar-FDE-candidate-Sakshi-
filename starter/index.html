Record the issue. Arrange service.

Record a service issue, request a technician or ask for a service credit. The assistant checks current records and policy before confirming an action.

This demo uses a fixed simulated date: 8 April 2030, 14:30 IST (India Standard Time, UTC+05:30). Each request starts with fresh test records. Service credits require a paid invoice and a recorded missed service commitment. Larger credits may need supervisor approval; pending approval does not mean a credit has been applied. Cancellation and rescheduling go to human review. A recorded ticket remains open if booking needs clarification or cannot finish.
